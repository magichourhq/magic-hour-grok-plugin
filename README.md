# Magic Hour for Grok Bot

Create and edit images and video from your agent with [Magic Hour](https://magichour.ai/mcp).

Connects to the Magic Hour MCP server at `https://mcp.magichour.ai/`.

## Setup
1. Install the plugin.
2. Connect your Magic Hour account. Sign in and choose **Allow** when prompted. If your client asks for an API key instead, create one in the [Developer Hub](https://magichour.ai) and paste it into the connect page (not into the chat). A free account includes daily credits.
3. Ask your agent to make something.

## Try it
- "Create a cinematic image of a crowded Thai night market with a neon Pad Thai sign."
- "Turn that image into a 5-second video."
- "Add stylish sunglasses to this photo and keep the result realistic."

## What it can change
Generations spend Magic Hour credits. The plugin can create and delete projects on your Magic Hour account. It does not read anything outside your Magic Hour account.

## Scope
Image and video generation and editing, including face swap, lip sync, talking photo and AI clothes changer. Speech generation and voice cloning are not available.
